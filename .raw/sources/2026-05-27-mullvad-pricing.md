# Mullvad Pricing Snapshot

Source: https://mullvad.net/en/pricing

Accessed on 2026-05-27.

## Captured Facts

- Mullvad presents one flat monthly price of EUR 5.
- The page showed EUR 5 as approximately USD 5.82 on 2026-05-27.
- The page says accounts can be used on up to 5 devices.
- The page says Mullvad does not support port forwarding.
- The page lists a 14-day money-back guarantee, except cash payments.

## Project Implications

- Cost modeling can start at EUR 5 per month per operator account.
- Device-limit language matters if the community package expects multiple machines.
- Port forwarding is not relevant to this download-orchestration use case.
