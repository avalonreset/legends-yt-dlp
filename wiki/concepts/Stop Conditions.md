# Stop Conditions

Stop conditions are events that require the app to pause or stop a batch instead of continuing automatically.

## Initial List

- Missing rights basis.
- Mullvad disconnected or ambiguous.
- Lockdown mode not verified.
- Captcha.
- Sign-in challenge.
- Explicit platform block.
- Repeated rate-limit signal.
- DRM, paywall, or access-control signal.
