# Glossary

## Batch

A planned set of URLs and settings to archive.

## Download Archive

A yt-dlp file that records completed downloads so repeated runs skip already-downloaded items.

## Fail Closed

Defaulting to no network action when safety checks are missing or ambiguous.

## Rights Basis

The documented reason the operator is allowed to download and preserve the content.

## Stop Condition

A signal that the batch must pause or stop for human review.
