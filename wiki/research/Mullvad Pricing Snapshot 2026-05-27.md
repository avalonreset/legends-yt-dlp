# Mullvad Pricing Snapshot 2026-05-27

Source: [[Mullvad Pricing]]

## Findings

- Mullvad's public pricing page shows a flat EUR 5 monthly price.
- On 2026-05-27 the page displayed approximate conversion to USD 5.82.
- One account is listed as usable on up to 5 devices.
- Mullvad states that port forwarding is not supported.

## Project Implications

- MVP docs can assume a simple Mullvad cost model.
- Community docs should tell users they need their own Mullvad account.
- Device-limit details should be rechecked before public release.
