# Rights and Consent Checklist

Before creating a batch, answer:

- Do you own the videos?
- Did the rights holder give permission?
- Is the content public-domain?
- Does the license permit local download and reuse?
- Are you using an official export path instead where appropriate?
- Are there account, paywall, DRM, or access-control restrictions?
- Is the intended use documented?

If the answer is unclear, do not run the batch.
