# Use Policy

Legends YT-DLP is for legitimate archival jobs only.

## Allowed

- Archiving your own videos.
- Archiving videos where the creator or rights holder gave permission.
- Archiving public-domain or appropriately licensed content when the license permits downloading.
- Creating a local backup where the operator has a lawful basis.
- Using Mullvad VPN as a privacy and leak-prevention layer.

## Not Allowed

- Downloading copyrighted videos without permission or lawful basis.
- Bypassing DRM, paywalls, private access controls, captchas, or login challenges.
- Rotating VPN endpoints to bypass throttling, bans, account controls, or IP blocks.
- Misrepresenting the tool as a way to avoid platform consequences.

## Release Copy Rule

Community-facing docs should say what the tool does plainly: safe, resumable, rights-aware video archiving behind a VPN privacy gate.
